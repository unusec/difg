2.	Familiar type classification:
Normal/Ordinary
- can't be listed on market
- can have enhanced rare skills
- can have legendary traits
- can be obtained from contracts that you buy from Nisza for 10k gold
- can be obtained from contracts that drop when you farm ow/dungeons/lairs/towers etc.
- can be bought from Nisza's shop in Westmarch
Aberrant
- can be sold on market
- can have legendary skills
- can have legendary traits
- can be bought from Nisza's shop in Westmarch
- can be bought from market
- can be obtained as an bonus extra pet(20% chance) when making a normal conversion, pet race is random.
3.	Familiar quality classification:
The way familiars look in game is based on their primary attribute, skills and traits! Attributes are most important when it comes to pets quality, they determine how powerful your pet skills, what traits are active or not and are a part on how your pet will look in game. If you can easily get a legendary skill and trait on a pet, legendary attributes are not so easy to come by, so always check for attributes values. 
Common: pet showing in game in it's first form, its icon will be grey if has an attribute  below 70
Magic: pet showing in game in it's first form, its icon will be blue if has an attribute of at least 70
Rare: second form of your pet, it's icon will be yellow if the pet has:
  - attribute of at least 120
  - rare skill;
Legendary: the 3rd and final form of your pet, it's icon will be red and will be unlocked if your pet has:
  - attribute of at least 170
  - legendary skill or enhanced rare skill
  - legendary trait
4.	Familiar's skills classification:
Skills that your familiar will have will look diferent based on their quality. Some skill are only legendary, others can be [rare, enhanced rare or legendary] and other skills are available in all forms. Based on your pet attributes your skills have their power increased, you can check this Google sheet for more info. 
Normal: blue skills
Rare: yellow skills
Enhanced Rare: yellow skills with a red like border
Legendary: red skills with a purple like border
5.	Familiar's traits:
Your pet can get traits based on his skills quality, meaning that a pet without an enhanced rare or legendary skill will not be able to get an advanced/legendary trait. Also traits are activated eighter by your pet primary attribute value when it comes to Common traits or by primary OR secondary attribute when it comes to Advanced/Legendary traits. Check this Google sheet for info on all traits and how you can stack them. 
The number of legendary traits is based on the number of legendary/enhanced skills your pet has:
1 E/L skill = max 1 leg trait
2,3 E/L skills = max 2 leg traits
4,5 E/L skills = max 3 leg traits
6 E/L skills = max 4 leg traits
Common traits:
- can't be stacked to increase their bonus values
- requires your pet main attribute to be 120 in order for them to be active.
Utility traits(categorized by game as common traits)
- active by default
- will give you the option to change your loadout, salvage or identify items.
- you can have more than 1 utility trait on the same pet
Advanced(legendary) traits:
- can be stacked if your bonded pets are different race and have the same trait active
- you need primary or secondary attribute of at least 170 in order for a legendary trait to be active. If you get multiple legendary traits your pet might need double lego attributes(of at least 170) to activate them all
6.	Conversions
If you want to preserve the race of your pet the other pets in the mix must be same race. If you want your resulting pet not to preserve a certain skill make sure the other pets in the mix don't have that skill and vice-versa. 
Converting normal(ordinary) familiars:
- is free
- preserves the attributes of the best pet in the mix(attributes wise)
- the result can't have less skill power(if your best pet, skill wise, has 6rare skills, the outcome can't be 5 leg skills but it can be 1 enhanced rare skill or more)
- 10% chance at an enhanced rare skill
- you have 20% chance on getting a new random race aberrant familiar with random attributes and skills
- UNVERIFIED: when the familiars to be converted have 3 enhanced rare skills, the new familiar has a 25% chance of carrying a Legendary skill.(I guess this refers to the new aberrant familiar)
- you can as a result get a normal pet with enhanced rare skills, that can have legendary traits
- if your normal pet has an enhanced rare skill, legendary trait and an attribute of at least 170 will be displayed in the 3rd and final form in game

21.	There are 3 types of traits: yellow (officially “common”), orange (officially “advanced”) and purple (officially “utility”). Yellow traits give a small bonus and don’t stack. For example, 4 distinct familiar species with yellow Maim each will give 1% crit chance all together, not different from 1 familiar with yellow Maim. Orange traits give bigger bonuses and stack. For example, 1 familiar with orange Maim gives 1% crit chance, 4 distinct familiar species with orange Maim together give 8% crit chance. The only difficult part is they must be different species. Stack orange traits from 4-5 different familiar species to get high stats.
 
22.	Traits are activated either by primary or secondary attributes of the familiar. You can click at the inactive trait to see what attribute it requires. For example, Varog traits can require either Agility or Insight. Yellow traits require attribute value 120 which is easy to get no matter what attribute. Orange traits require attribute value 170 which is harder to get. Preferably you need orange traits activated by the primary attribute because Varog with 170+ Agility is relatively cheap, 20-50k platinum, but Varog with 170+ Insight is very expensive, 200-300k platinum. Primary (good) attributes for each familiar: Quakestrider - Ferocity; Myrrjen - Ferocity; Stormlost - Agility; Varog - Agility; Broodtalon - Insight; Skitterwight - Insight; Bramblestalker - Insight; Duskprowler - Resilience; Ashsweeper - Resilience; Prioritize orange traits activated by the primary attribute.

You can notice that 4L2R is worse than 4L0R. Or that 6L can generate 4 orange traits while 6E can generate max 3 orange traits and even 3 orange traits with a low 40% chance. The reason is that the yellow traits are coded to have the highest priority. If the familiar generates 3 orange traits and 2 yellow traits while only 4 spots are available - the game prioritizes 2 yellow traits. It is even worse for ordinary familiars that can generate 1-3 yellow traits no matter how many enhanced rare skills or rare skills there are. Even the extremely rare 6E generates 1-3 yellow traits instead of 4 orange 0 yellow traits as expected. Similarly for the purple utility traits. Yellow traits take priority over purple traits, and purple traits take priority over orange traits. This is bad coding which makes ordinary familiars have less orange traits than they could have. As well as it makes 4L2R worse than 4L0R and 4L1R. Because yellow traits were coded to have the highest priority 
 
27.	The maximum number of traits is 6: 
•	4 slots for yellow, orange, purple traits.
•	1 slot is reserved for the amalgam indicator.
•	1 slot is reserved for cosmetic buffs such as fishing trait of the murloc cosmetics.
For orange, yellow, purple traits there are max 4 slots. Even if in theory a familiar can generate 4 orange, 3 yellow, 3 purple traits, only 4 of them will be allowed, by the current priority in this case it will be 3 yellow 1 purple traits.
