# Skills & Attributes Guide

## 1. Skill Quality Tiers

Skills dictate the passive and active combat benefits provided by your familiar. They are divided into 4 visual quality tiers:

- **Normal (Blue)**: Basic familiar skills.
- **Rare (Yellow)**: Enhanced skill effects.
- **Enhanced Rare (Yellow with Red Border)**: Found on Ordinary familiars; grants access to Legendary Traits.
- **Legendary (Red with Purple Border)**: Exclusive to Aberrant familiars; provides maximum combat bonuses.

> [!TIP]
> Attribute values directly increase the effectiveness of familiar skills. High attribute numbers scale skill power significantly.

---

## 2. Traits Activation & Attribute Tiers

Traits grant stat boosts (such as Crit Chance or PvP Damage Reduction). They require specific attribute thresholds to become active:

- **Common Traits**:
  - Require **120** in main attribute to activate.
  - Do **not** stack with identical traits across bonded familiars.
- **Utility Traits (Purple)**:
  - Active by default.
  - Provide out-of-combat utilities (e.g. Remote Salvage, Identify, Armory Loadout changes).
- **Advanced / Legendary Traits (Orange)**:
  - Require **170** in Primary or Secondary attribute to activate.
  - Can be **stacked** across up to 5 distinct familiar species.

---

## 3. "Undercover Legendary" Familiars

An **Undercover Legendary** is a familiar that possesses a **Legendary Attribute (170+)**, but lacks the necessary Legendary Skill or Trait required to unlock its 3rd in-game visual form.

Because its icon remains blue or yellow, sellers on the market or Nisza's shop often fail to notice them!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Always inspect pet attribute numbers!** A cheap pet with 170+ attribute value is extremely valuable as an attribute donor for your main combat familiar.
