# Traits Stacking & Priority Guide

Trait stacking is one of the most powerful endgame mechanics for pet optimization in Diablo Immortal.

---

## 1. Trait Categories & Stacking Rules

| Trait Color | Category | Behavior | Attribute Req. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | Common | **Does NOT stack** across pets. 4 pets with Yellow Maim still grant only 1% Crit Chance total. | Main Attribute ≥ 120 |
| 🟣 **Purple** | Utility | Active by default. Grants Remote Salvage, Identify, Armory Loadouts. | None |
| 🟠 **Orange** | Advanced | **STACKS** when bonded across **distinct familiar species**. 4 distinct species with Orange Maim grant 8% Crit Chance! | Primary/Secondary Attribute ≥ 170 |

---

## 2. Primary Attributes per Familiar Species

Always target Orange traits activated by the species' **Primary Attribute** for cost efficiency:

- **Ferocity**: Quakestrider, Myrrjen
- **Agility**: Stormlost, Varog
- **Insight**: Broodtalon, Skitterwight, Bramblestalker
- **Resilience**: Duskprowler, Ashsweeper

---

## 3. Trait Priority Coding Mechanics

> [!WARNING]
> Familiars have a maximum of **4 trait slots** for Yellow, Orange, and Purple traits combined.
> Game priority places **Yellow traits FIRST**, which can overwrite desired Orange traits!
> - Priority Order: `Yellow (Common) > Purple (Utility) > Orange (Advanced)`

---

## 4. Popular Meta Trait Stacking Setups

### 🗡️ PvE Setup: 4 Maim & 4 Bestial Might
- **Stats**: +8% Crit Hit Chance (Maim) & Primary Attack Damage (Bestial Might).
- **Required Familiars**:
  - Duskprowler
  - Varog
  - Broodtalon
  - Quakestrider
  - (Optional 5th): Bramblestalker with 5th Bestial Might.

### 🛡️ PvP Setup: 5 Prescience & Defensive Traits
- **Stats**: Major Damage Reduction from players.
- **Required Familiars**:
  - Primary Aberrant: **Ashsweeper** (with Savior & Prescience)
  - 4 Bonded Familiars: Varog, Broodtalon, Bramblestalker, Myrrjen.

### ⚡ PvP Offensive Setup: 4 Fiendish Haste
- **Stats**: Increased damage dealt to players.
- **Required Familiars**:
  - Primary Aberrant: **Duskprowler** (with Savior & Fiendish Haste)
  - 3 Bonded Familiars: Quakestrider, Stormlost, Skitterwight.
