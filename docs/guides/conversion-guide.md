# Conversions & Conversion Stones Guide

Converting familiars allows you to reroll skills, attributes, and race traits. Understanding when to use **Conversion Stones** is critical to avoid wasting high-value pets.

---

## 1. Converting Normal (Ordinary) Familiars

- **Cost**: **FREE** (0 Platinum).
- **Attribute Preservation**: Preserves the highest attribute stats of the best pet in the mix.
- **Skill Floor**: Resulting familiar cannot lose skill power tier (e.g. converting a 6-rare pet will not degrade to 5 blue skills).
- **Aberrant Chance**: 20% chance to yield a bonus **Aberrant Familiar** of a random race.
- **Enhanced Skill Chance**: 10% chance to roll an Enhanced Rare skill.

---

## 2. Converting Aberrant Familiars

- **Cost**: **2,000 Platinum**.
- Higher attribute pets increase the chance of rolling Legendary Skills:
  - For a 5-legendary skill pet: ~0.018% chance on legendary quality.
  - For a 6-legendary skill pet: ~0.0023% chance.

---

## 3. Using Conversion Stones vs Without Stones

> [!CAUTION]
> **Rule #1**: ALWAYS use Conversion Stones if ANY pet in the conversion mix has an attribute of **170 or higher**!

### With Conversion Stones
- **Preserves Attributes**: Retains the highest attribute stats from your donor pet.
- **Preserves Skill Power**: Guarantees skill power tier is not lost.
- **Stone Cost Scale**:
  - 1 Leg Skill: 2 Stones
  - 2 Leg Skills: 8 Stones
  - 3 Leg Skills: 30 Stones
  - 4 Leg Skills: 40 Stones
  - 5 Leg Skills: 50 Stones
  - 6 Leg Skills: 60 Stones

### Without Conversion Stones
- **Randomizes Attributes**: Completely resets attributes to random values (even if you input three 170+ attribute pets!).
- **Skill Power Risk**: Can lose up to 1 skill power tier.

---

## 4. How to Transfer Legendary Attributes Across Races

If you have an **Undercover Legendary** pet (170+ attribute) on a race you don't want, follow these steps to transfer stats to your desired pet:

### Method A: Using Normal Familiars
1. Transfer legendary attributes to a normal familiar (if currently on aberrant).
2. Change race by converting with 2 normal pets of your desired target race.
3. Use **Attribute Exchange** at Nisza NPC to transfer stats to your target pet.

### Method B: Using Aberrant Familiars
1. Convert the aberrant pet **WITH STONES** together with 2 aberrant pets of your target race.
2. There is a **66% chance** the resulting pet changes to the target race.
3. Perform **Attribute Exchange** at Nisza NPC for 3,000 Platinum.
