# Nisza NPC Guide

Nisza is the primary Familiar Merchant NPC located in Westmarch.

---

## 1. Nisza Services Overview

- **Stock Refresh**: Stock refreshes randomly every **4 to 30 minutes** between **10:00 and 00:00 server time**. Pets remain available for 4 hours before expiring.
- **Weekly Contracts**: Buy up to **3 Contracts per week for 10,000 Gold each**.
- **Attribute Exchange**: Swap attribute stats between same-race familiars for **3,000 Platinum** (non-destructive operation).
- **Expeditions**: Send familiars on dispatch towers, Iben Fahd, and Shadow contracts.

---

## 2. Spotting Undercover Legendaries at Nisza

Because Nisza's shop stock never displays Enhanced Rare or Legendary Skills, any familiar in her stock with a **Primary Attribute ≥ 170** is an **Undercover Legendary** selling at standard price!

Be fast—other pet master buyers check Nisza constantly during refresh windows!
